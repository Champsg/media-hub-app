# VidKwaii Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 4 September 2026

Thank you for using VidKwaii ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what
information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. By using the App
you agree to this policy.

## 1. What VidKwaii does

VidKwaii is a free, ad-supported media utility that lets you save public
Instagram videos and reels you are allowed to download to your device for
offline viewing. It also includes an in-app browser for Instagram media pages
and an offline player.

## 2. Information we handle

**Links you provide.** When you paste or enter an Instagram link, the link is
sent to our backend service so it can fetch the public media metadata (title,
thumbnail, available formats) and resolve the download URLs. The link is used
only to fulfil your request.

**Downloaded media.** Videos and audio you download are stored in app-managed
storage on your device and exported to your device's gallery or music folders
when the app saves them. We do not upload or copy your downloads to our
servers.

**Advertising data.** The App uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation to
show interstitial ads. Like most advertising SDKs, it may collect identifiers
such as the Android advertising ID, device model/OS version, IP address, and
limited app-activity signals (for example, that an ad was shown or closed) in
order to deliver, measure, and protect ads. This happens in accordance with
the [Unity privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy) and
[Unity LevelPlay policies](https://developers.is.com/).

We do not combine ad identifiers with your saved downloads, and we do not use
analytics cookies or any other third-party analytics SDK.

## 3. Third-party processing

- **Ad partners:** ads are mediated through Unity LevelPlay. Individual ad
  networks reached through mediation may process data under their own privacy
  policies and may use the advertising ID.
- **Instagram / Meta:** when you request a download, our backend retrieves
  publicly available content from Instagram using automated tools. Instagram
  may log that request under its own privacy policy.
- **Backend hosting:** our extraction service is hosted by a cloud provider.
  Temporary files created to fulfil a request are deleted automatically
  within 30 minutes. We do not retain extracted content.

## 4. Encryption and security

Your links and download requests are transmitted to our backend over the
Internet. The current production backend is reachable over **HTTP (not
encrypted in transit)**. We recommend HTTPS and are working toward serving
the backend exclusively over HTTPS; this policy and the Play Store Data
safety declaration will be updated when that happens.

Downloaded files stay in your device's app storage and public media folders.
We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Permissions

- **Internet:** required to fetch links, download media, and load ads.
- **Media access (Android 8/9):** a one-time storage permission is requested
  only when you save a downloaded file into your gallery. The App does not
  browse or read your other media on Android 10 and above.
- **Keep screen on:** used while videos play so playback is not interrupted.
- **Advertising ID:** used by the LevelPlay ad SDK as described above.

## 6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process
the personal data of children. We recommend parental supervision because the
in-app browser can display user-generated content.

## 7. Your choices

- **Ads:** on Android you can reset or opt out of the advertising ID at any
  time under Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device. This limits ad
  personalization but does not stop the App from showing ads.
- **Deletion:** you can delete downloaded media from the App's Library at any
  time, or clear the App's data in your device settings.
- **Contact:** to ask about, access, or request deletion of any data you
  believe we hold, contact us at **privacy@vidkwaii.app**.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new
effective date.
